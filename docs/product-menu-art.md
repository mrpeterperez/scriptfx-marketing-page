# Product menu illustrations

## Current object-only direction

The live menus use the six `*-icon.png` files in `public/art/products/`, generated with the built-in image-generation tool. People illustrations below are retained as earlier explorations and are no longer displayed. The current reusable prompt and feature subjects live in `src/lib/site/art-direction.ts` and are available under Design Kit > Illustrations with a copy button. Use dark graphite on off-white paper; do not include people or invert the colors.

## Earlier exploration

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the approved paper storyboard as the style reference. Assets are stored in `public/art/products/` and displayed through Next Image in the desktop and mobile Products menus.

## Prompts

### screenplay-editor

ONE square thumbnail illustration for screenplay-editor: spiky-haired writer with pencil and script at desk. Match attached approved rough storyboard EXACTLY: simple rounded cartoon heads, dot eyes, tiny mouths, crude hands, messy scratchy gray graphite pencil lines, sparse hatching, simple casual clothes. DARK PENCIL ON LIGHT OFF-WHITE PAPER. Faces and skin must be light paper, not dark. No inverted or negative effect, no black background, no realistic anatomy, no polished rendering. Single close composition, no grid, no text, no border, minimal background, useful at small menu thumbnail size.
